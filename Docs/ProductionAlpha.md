# Production Alpha: Operation Redline

The Sprint 4 deliverable: one complete ground mission that shows what the final game plays like. Blue (the
Heroes) push through a valley held by Red (the Villains). Space and flight are left out on purpose. The
mission teaches every on-foot mechanic in order, from the simplest to the most demanding, and finishes with
a battle that uses all of them at once.

- **Map:** `/Game/Metalization/Maps/MZ_ProductionAlpha` (game mode `AMetalAlphaGameMode`: the action camera
  plus the full HUD).
- **Title screen:** `/Game/Metalization/Maps/MZ_Title`, the packaged game's start map. **Start Mission** opens
  the first entry in Level Select, which is this mission.
- **Generated by:** `Tools/AssetPipeline/ue_setup_alpha.py` (`build_alpha`, `build_title`). The maps are
  rebuilt from the script, so make layout changes there. To rebuild only these two maps:

  ```powershell
  $env:MZ_LEVELS="alpha,title"; powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\AssetPipeline\run_pipeline.ps1 -SkipBlender -SkipImport
  ```

## Gameplay loop

Move up → read the radio and the control tip → use the new mechanic → clear the objective. That repeats 13
times, with checkpoints along the way. The player starts with only the Laser and no grenades. The Repeater,
grenades, sniper and rocket launcher are handed out as they become relevant. Dying respawns the player at
the last checkpoint with their loadout. Finishing shows a results panel (time, kills, deaths, accuracy) with
Retry or the menu.

## The mission, beat by beat

The map runs west to east (+X). It's about 400 m long and 32 m wide, with three ground heights: the valley
floor (0 m), the bridge ledge (2.5 m) and the clifftop plateau (12.5 m).

| # | Area | Teaches | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blue camp | Move / look | Start among the tents and Blue troopers. Command gives the briefing. |
| 2 | Supply trench | **Sprint** | A 50 m trench. An artillery barrage (`AMetalBarrage`) lands shells just behind the player: walking gets you clipped, sprinting outruns it. |
| 3 | Barricades | Jump | Waist-high barricades, then a shallow ditch you can jump back out of. |
| 4 | Collapsed bridge | Double jump | A 4 m gap to a ledge 2.5 m higher. Only a double jump reaches it. Falling in leads to a ramp back up. **Checkpoint.** |
| 5 | Cliff | **Anti-grav lift** | A 10 m cliff. The lift (`AMetalGravLift`) carries you up and drops you on the plateau. |
| 6 | Forward post | Shooting, **weapon-arm swap** | A Blue squad holds the sandbags while a Red squad waits beyond sight range. A Repeater lies by the sandbags, and picking it up plays the first acted-out arm swap. Pushing forward pulls the Reds into a firefight with you and the Blues. |
| 7 | Bunker | Grenades | Three Reds hunker behind a chest-high wall, with only their heads showing. Grab grenades and lob one (it arcs onto the crosshair); the EMP blast stuns them all. |
| 8 | Patrol yard | **Sneaking** | A walled yard with four patrols under red spotlights and low walls. A crawl pipe on the left only fits a crouched robot. Suspicion meters show who's getting curious; being spotted brings the yard running (no mission fail). **Checkpoint.** |
| 9 | Sniper perch | Lift + **sniper** | A lift to a tower perch with the sniper rifle. Three Red marksmen with red laser sights cover the valley from their towers. |
| 10 | Shield gate | Hacking | A Red-only energy fence. The Hack Tether goes through it to take over the guard. |
| 11 | Gate console | Consoles | Walk the hijacked Red to the Red-only console. Reds barely suspect one of their own. The console opens the gate and the Blue reinforcement hangar. |
| 12 | Command yard | Everything | Blue reinforcements storm in. Reds, a catwalk reached by lift and a Brute commander. A rocket launcher waits by the gate. |
| 13 | Extraction | — | Reach the pad, then the results screen. |

## New mechanics

| Mechanic | Controls | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint | Hold Shift / L3 | `AMetalBotCharacter::SetWantsSprint`, `TickLocomotion`; `UMetalBotArchetype::SprintSpeed` (player 1000 cm/s, walk 600) |
| Sneak (crouch) | Hold Ctrl, or toggle with C / R3 | `SetSneaking` (engine crouch with a shorter capsule), `UMetalBotArchetype::CrouchSpeed` (260) |
| Hearing and suspicion | — | `AMetalBotAIController::ReportNoise`, `HearNoise`, `BuildSuspicion`; HUD `DrawAwareness` |
| Sniper rifle | RMB scope, LMB fire | `WD_Sniper`: `AimFOV` 16, `AimArmLengthScale` 0 (eye level), `bScopeOverlay`, `HipSpreadDegrees` 5; HUD `DrawScope` |
| Anti-gravity lift | Walk in | `AMetalGravLift` (`Source/Metalization/Game/MetalGravLift.*`) |
| Acted-out weapon swap | 1–6 / wheel / Y | `EquipWeapon` → `TickWeaponSwap`; poses in `UMetalBotAnimInstance` |
| Jetpack | fires on the double jump | `BuildJetpack` / `FireJetpack` / `TickJetpack`; archetype `bHasJetpack`, rig `JetpackOffset` / `JetpackSpread` |

### Jetpack

The player wears two thruster pods on the back. They fire only on the mid-air (double) jump: a ~0.55 s
burst of stretched glowing flames, a warm light, an ignition puff and the thruster sound. The flames die
as soon as the robot lands. The parts are engine shapes attached to the pelvis (they follow the
double-jump flip). Flames are sized relative to the pivot's "unit" scale, because the imported skeleton's
bone sockets carry a scale; sizing them with absolute relative scales made them 100x too big.

### Hacking: letting go

Disconnecting (F, the timer running out, or the player's body dying) no longer destroys the hijacked
robot. A robot that still has health goes back to its own side: its paint returns, it gets a fresh AI
controller and is stunned for `RebootTime` (2 s, `AMetalPlayerController`) while it reboots. Only losing
its health destroys a hacked robot, so a hijack is a tactical loan, not a free kill.

### Weapon-arm swap

The player's gun *is* the right forearm's attachment (bonemerged to `weapon_bone`). A swap takes 0.8 s
(`UMetalBotArchetype::WeaponSwapDuration`), and the robot can't fire during it:

1. **Reach (0–0.28):** the left hand reaches across and grips the gun arm. Both forearms twist.
2. **Off (0.28):** the gun comes off. The forearm ends in a bare mount, and the gun is now in the left
   hand (`CarriedGun`).
3. **Rack (0.5):** the arm swings over the shoulder, leaves the old gun on the back rack and pulls the new
   one off it.
4. **On (0.8):** the arm swings back round, snaps the new gun onto the forearm (with a recoil "clunk"), and
   the hand returns.

The two most recently stowed guns stay visible on the back rack (`RackGunA/B`). The carried and racked guns
reuse the held meshes: `PlaceWeaponOnBone` puts the gun's grip (its `weapon_bone` in the bind pose) on the
hand or the back. Changing your mind mid-swap retargets it. AI robots and respawn loadouts still swap
instantly.

### Stealth model

- **Sight.** Spotting the player isn't instant any more. While the player is in view, a robot's suspicion
  fills from 0 to 1:
  - **Distance.** About 0.4 s up close, 3.5 s at the edge of sight.
  - **Stance.** Sneaking makes it 2.5× slower; sprinting makes it 1.7× faster.
  - **Shooting.** Firing in the last 1.5 s makes it 4× faster.
  - **Disguise.** A hijacked robot of the watcher's own side makes it 4× slower.
- **What a sneaking robot gets.** It is only seen at about 60% of the normal range, and isn't felt behind
  the back.
- **What suspicion does.** At half, the robot walks over to look. When full, it attacks and alerts its squad.
- **Other robots.** Robots that aren't the player are fought on sight, as before.
- **Noise.** Every noise is heard within its radius, and the hearer goes to investigate:
  - footsteps: walking 4.5 m, sprinting 13 m, sneaking silent;
  - gunshots: the weapon's `NoiseRadius` (sniper 45 m);
  - explosions: 18 m.
- **HUD.** A yellow "?" with a meter over curious robots, and a red "!" for 2.5 s when one spots you.

## UI added for the alpha

- **Title screen.** `AMetalTitleGameMode` / `AMetalTitlePlayerController`. The menu is `UMetalPauseMenu` in
  title mode (Start Mission / Level Select / Controls / Quit) over a slow orbit of a Blue-vs-Red diorama.
- **Pause menu.** Continue, Restart Mission, Level Select, Controls, Title Screen and Quit.
- **HUD text size.** All HUD text is drawn at twice its font size (`AMetalHUD::TextScale`); panels are laid
  out from measured text so nothing overlaps. Menus, the big centre banner and the "?"/"!" icons keep their
  size.
- **HUD additions.**
  - **Control tips:** a card under the objective (`FMetalObjective::Hint`).
  - **Objective marker:** with distance, pinned to the screen edge when off screen.
  - **Stance line:** SNEAKING / SPRINTING.
  - **Scope overlay.**
  - **Suspicion markers.**
  - **Swap state:** "SWAPPING ARM..." on the weapon panel.
- **Results panel.** Time, enemies destroyed, times scrapped, accuracy (hitscan shots that hit a robot);
  ENTER plays again, ESC opens the menu.
- **Faction colours.** Shooter, Soldier, Brute and Marksman carry Blue and Red paint
  (`UMetalBotArchetype::TeamColors`). Allies placed on the Heroes team and hijacked enemies turn blue.

## Testing (Test Candidate 01)

Each new beat has an autopilot smoke test that drives it and records a log and screenshots:

| Scenario | Checks |
|---|---|
| `alpha` | Sprint down the trench (1000 cm/s) without being hit by the barrage; jump the first barricade |
| `bridge` | Double jump onto the higher ledge; checkpoint and next objective |
| `lift` | Ride the cliff lift from 3.3 m to the plateau at 13 m |
| `swap` | Pick up the Repeater; swap arms three times (side camera, shots at each beat) |
| `bunker` | Lob EMP grenades onto the Reds behind the wall, then shoot them |
| `sneak` | Crawl the pipe past the yard patrols without being spotted (logs every robot's suspicion) |
| `sniper` | Scope in from the perch and drop a marksman with one shot |
| `hack` | Hijack the guard through the fence, open the gate, Blue reinforcements engage |
| `title` | Title screen and menu |

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\AssetPipeline\smoke_test.ps1 -Scenario sneak
```

Test Candidate 01 is the packaged Development build: `RunUAT BuildCookRun ... -archivedirectory=<project>`
(see [Metalization_Plan.md](Metalization_Plan.md)). Add `-Packaged` to any scenario to run it there.

## Known issues / next steps

- **Visuals.** Everything is still graybox, and the automated captures render with a strong blue cast (the
  Outpost does too).
- **AI.** It doesn't use lifts or crouch.
- **Feel.** Balance (damage, suspicion times, barrage spacing) is from automated runs only and needs
  hand playtesting (Testing Round 1).
- **Swap.** The swap poses are tuned for the Player rig only. Other robots swap instantly.
